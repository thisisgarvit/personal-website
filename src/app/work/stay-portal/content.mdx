Five apartments, rented by the full day, the half day, or the hour, run
entirely from a phone. Stay Portal is the booking system that replaced the
spreadsheet holding that operation together.

## Situation

The operation ran on a Google Sheets workbook — one tab per room, edited by
hand. Bookings arrive from Airbnb, offline walk-ins, and referrals, and they
do not behave like vacation-rental bookings: one room can turn over several
times in a single day across full-day, half-day, and hourly stays. Tools
built around one nightly booking per room cannot model the actual business.

The workbook's failure modes were predictable: accidental double-bookings,
no at-a-glance view of what is happening today, pending payments nobody
chased because nothing tracked them, and zero analytics. The people running
it are non-technical and work from their phones.

## The bet

Build the smallest product that fixes those four failures, and make the
expensive one — double-booking — structurally impossible rather than merely
discouraged. Ship it phone-first as an installable web app, because that is
where the operators already work. And do not ask anyone to abandon the
spreadsheet: mirror the product's data back into a familiar workbook every
night, so trusting the new system never requires giving up the old one.

## Decisions and exclusions

- **Overlap prevention is layered, and the strongest layer is the deepest.**
  The database enforces an exclusion constraint on room + time range, so two
  conflicting bookings cannot both exist; the booking form warns live while
  the operator types; and the API rejects a clash with the conflict attached.
  A UI warning alone still loses the race between two phones.
- **The Google Sheets mirror is an adoption bridge, not a legacy crutch.**
  A nightly one-way sync writes bookings back into a workbook with one tab
  per room — the exact shape the operators already trust. They keep the
  familiar view and get a free daily backup; the product stays the single
  source of truth.
- **Two operator accounts, nothing more.** No roles, no admin console, no
  team features. The business has two people in it.
- **The browser never talks to the database directly** — every read and
  write goes through the app's own API.
- **The public demo is isolated from production by design.** Production
  holds real guest names and mobile numbers, so the demo environment and
  every screenshot on this page use synthetic data instead. Showing the real
  thing was never on the table.

## Evidence

- 484 historical bookings imported from the old workbook across the five
  apartments — the product started with the operation's full history, not an
  empty state.
- ₹0 per month in infrastructure to run.
- The nightly Sheets mirror has a second job: it is the operation's daily
  backup.
