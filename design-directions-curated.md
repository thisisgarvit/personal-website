# Curated Design Directions — merged & judged

Judge: Claude (orchestrator), merging two independent scouting pools with zero site overlap:
- `design-refs-claude.md` — 14 refs, skews toward humor devices & playful mechanics
- `design-refs-codex.md` — 10 refs, skews toward operator credibility & utility interactions

**Validated principle (both pools converged independently):** one committed concept + restrained execution + a single self-aware joke. Personality only lands after role/scope/evidence are obvious within 10 seconds. Losing pattern: generic template, no voice, or animation that hides the work.

---

## Direction 1 — "Quiet Operator, One Killer Toy"  ⭐ judge's recommendation

Restrained, tasteful base layer with real outcomes visible fast — plus ONE signature interactive quirk device that makes founders smile.

**Reference stack:**
- Base restraint & microcopy wink: **mc1ane.com** ("Get in touch" → "I'm touched"), **sj.land** (number-key nav, easter eggs)
- The contained toy: **pacolui.com** (optional hand-tracking canvas — "Maybe later"), **marco.fyi** (iMessage contact page)
- Outcome credibility: **borisverks.com** (check-marked metrics as visual material)

**Why it wins for Garvit:** hits all three personas without trade-off — HR gets aesthetics, product leads get taste + evidence, founders get the smile. The quirk is contained, so it never blocks the résumé read.

## Direction 2 — "Comedy as Credibility"

Copy-led; the joke IS the skill demo. The site's spine is one persuasion mechanic.

**Reference stack:** **getcoleman.com** (the hard-sell slider — for a PM: a "TL;DR ↔ Full PRD" depth slider rewriting the page), **sriponnada.com** ("my (actual) work / my (other) work"), **fromjason.xyz** (editorial voice).

**Why/why not:** strongest founder-smile potential and most unique; highest execution risk — the joke must be genuinely good and the writing carries everything. HR persona served least.

## Direction 3 — "Operator's Field Notes"

Dark utility command-surface. Every pixel performs a job; personality lives in dry microcopy and keyboard-driven interactions.

**Reference stack:** **justinfarrugia.com** (press C to copy email, availability status, task-level project titles), **borisverks.com** (metrics-dense, brutalist-warm), **molly.info** (one perfectly edited paragraph as homepage).

**Why/why not:** founders and product leads first; reads startup-native and fast. Least whimsical of the four — quirk budget is small by design. HR aesthetics depend on very disciplined execution.

## Direction 4 — "Editorial Product Garden"

The site as a body of product thinking — teardowns, case studies, notes — typography-first, quirk via curation and voice.

**Reference stack:** **robinrendle.com** (portfolio-as-accumulated-thought), **chester.how** (whole-person garden: work + hobbies + reading tracker), **elizabethgoodspeed.com** (editorial flat-plan index), **havananguyen.com** (doodle icon system, repeated mantra device).

**Why/why not:** best fit for the actual content (the Notion export IS product thinking pieces) and strongest product-lead signal; weakest 10-second founder immediacy unless the landing is very sharp.

## Signature quirk device candidates (pluggable into any direction)

1. **Depth slider** — "Elevator pitch ↔ Full PRD" slider that rewrites the intro/case-study depth (from getcoleman)
2. **iMessage contact card** — text-bubble contact UI with real feedback states (from marco.fyi)
3. **Keyboard-first nav** — number keys / press-C-to-copy-email, with a visible hint (from sj.land + justinfarrugia)
4. **(actual)/(other) work split** — structural joke separating PM work from life (from sriponnada)
5. **Visitor-ambient touch** — tiny guestbook / "wave hi" that persists across visitors (from spencer.place)
6. **Joke project with real execution** — one absurd-but-shipped mini artifact (from gkoberger's Stacksort)
