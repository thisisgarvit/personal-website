# Garvit Portfolio — Underrated Design Reference Scout (Claude pool)

Research pass: 17 July 2026. All URLs verified live via WebFetch except chester.how (up, rate-limits bots).

## Cluster 1: "Comedy as Credibility" — copy-led humor where the joke IS the skill demo

### ⭐ Joe Coleman — getcoleman.com
Freelance copywriter. The hard-sell slider: a slider from "Less Hard Sell" to "More Hard Sell" rewrites the entire homepage pitch through ~60 gradations. The mechanic demonstrates the craft instead of claiming it — best "show don't tell" device found. For a PM: imagine a slider from "elevator pitch" to "full PRD". Animated skateboarding dogs punctuate bold statements. Personas: Founders, Product leads, HR.

### ⭐ Sri Ponnada — sriponnada.com
PM by day, Brooklyn stand-up comic by night. Closest proof that "quirky-but-credible PM site" works. "my (actual) work" vs "my (other) work" — one structural joke reframes the site. Personality carried by voice, not effects. Personas: Founders, Product leads, HR.

### Jason Velazquez — fromjason.xyz
Writer, "cheese danish sommelier." Editorial typography with dropcaps, indie-press aesthetic, sections named "schemes/plots". Personas: Founders, Product leads.

### Samuel Faith — samuelfaith.com
Text-first single page; dry boundary-setting copy as design feature; credibility through candor. Personas: Founders, Product leads.

## Cluster 2: "Playful Desktop / Interactive Toy" — one spatial metaphor, fully committed

### ⭐ Marco Cornacchia — marco.fyi
Product designer (ex-Figma). iMessage-styled contact page ("Text Me" bubbles with real feedback states). Micro-animations with sound, mock mini-OS, projects as live interactive mockups in device frames. "Fewer, but better things." Personas: all three.

### ⭐ Greg Koberger — gkoberger.com
Founder of ReadMe. Illustrated-desk homepage: clicking objects navigates to subpages. Joke projects with real execution (Stacksort, the xkcd gag). Humor backed by craft. Personas: Founders (founder-to-founder), Product leads, HR.

### Spencer Chang — spencer.place
Site as "a living space actively shaped by visitors" — change the lights, leave a note. Steal the guestbook/ambient-visitor idea, not the artist register. Personas: Founders, Product leads.

### Chus Margallo — chusmargallo.space
Full retro-OS portfolio (draggable windows) — the maximalist calibration point. Personas: HR, Founders.

## Cluster 3: "Quiet Luxury With One Joke" — restrained, one wink in the microcopy

### ⭐ McLane Teitel — mc1ane.com
Product designer at Shopify. Near-silent minimal layout, huge project imagery — then one microcopy joke: click "Get in touch" → "I'm touched / Email copied." The exact quirk-to-restraint ratio for recruiter + product-lead personas. Personas: Product leads, HR, Founders.

### SJ Zhang — sj.land
Design lead at Coinbase. Number-key shortcuts (1–0) for nav; "Aesthetic Goods" boutique; "Reset onboarding" easter egg — personality through curation, not decoration. Personas: Product leads, HR.

### Masahito Leo Takeuchi — ma5a.com
Sparse site warmed by a hand-drawn bunny mascot and one disarming line. Cookie banner reads "ok / no thanks". Personas: HR, Founders.

## Cluster 4: "Editorial / Digital Garden" — the site as a body of thinking

### Robin Rendle — robinrendle.com
Designer-writer at Apple. 900+ Notes + long-form Stories; portfolio-as-accumulated-thought. Typography-first, proves taste without a single animation. Personas: Product leads, Founders.

### Chester — chester.how
Digital garden mixing work with climbing/film/plants + live reading tracker — whole-person architecture. Personas: Founders, HR.

### Havana Nguyen — havananguyen.com
Hand-drawn doodle icon system; repeated mantra in varying typographic treatments; self-aware case-study copy. Personas: HR, Product leads.

## Synthesis
Winning pattern: one committed concept + restrained execution + a single self-aware joke; voice-driven copy over effects. Losing pattern: generic template + no voice. Promising synthesis: Cluster-3 restraint as base, one Cluster-2 interactive device, Cluster-4 editorial depth for product leads.
