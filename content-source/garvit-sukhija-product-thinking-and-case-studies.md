**Conceptualizing the Future of Browsing**

### 📆 Agentic Calendar

**Reimagining Calendars as Autonomous Scheduling Agents**

What happens when your calendar stops being a grid you fill and starts being an agent that manages your time proactively? An intelligent agentic layer on top of existing tools.

> 📦 **What's inside**
>
> - Competitive landscape (Reclaim, Motion, Clockwise)
> - Integration architecture (Slack, Gmail, Jira, Uber)
> - 4 core features
> - Wireframes deployed on Vercel
> - Detailed mockup walkthrough

> 💡 **Key insight:** The agent classifies your time into creative, operational, and restorative timespace. Subsequently schedules around your natural rhythms instead of forcing you to block every minute.

→ **Read the full case study**

📆 [Agentic Calendar](agentic-calendar.md)

**A Product Teardown of iOS Dynamic Island**
